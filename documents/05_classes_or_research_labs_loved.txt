Title: All majors: What are some classes or research labs you loved being in?
Source URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/uichicago/comments/hkziru/all_majors_what_are_some_classes_or_research_labs/
Source type: Reddit thread from r/uichicago
Topic: Student experiences in UIC research labs, classes, professors, and research opportunities

Cleaned content:
Original post: Thought this would be nice to see what classes might be interesting for people not in your major to take. Feel free to share favorite professors or clubs/opportunities too.

A student wrote that Dr. Zinser's social and emotional learning lab was a positive experience. The student said people in the lab were welcoming and the environment felt like home. They described joining as a freshman, being mentored, and being allowed to work at their own pace.

A computer science major wrote that any course can be interesting if a student is willing to put in effort and learn. They suggested CS 151/301 for theory-oriented students, CS 251/342 for hands-on programming, and CS 261 for machine code or assembly. The student also mentioned that they later received a research spot in machine learning with Professor McCarty.

In a follow-up, the same student said they took CS 151 with McCarty, did well, went to office hours, and became known to the professor. They then contacted him after finals and said that after taking CS 251 and a statistics class, students could try to get themselves known to a professor and then follow up by email. They described this as showing dedication and seriousness.
